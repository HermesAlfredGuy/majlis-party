under **CC0 1.0** — public domain, commercial use allowed, no credit required. We credit anyway.

Only the files actually used are copied here, per the library's rule. The originals live in
`/opt/data/assets/kenney/` and are never edited in place.

**Restyling.** Nothing here ships as stock Kenney: `public/majlis-glb.js` remaps every material
by name to the party's palette (deep wine upholstery, walnut, brass, lantern gold), adds contact
shadows and lantern light, draws its own Sadu rug, and tints each avatar's body to its team
colour. Kenney's bright toy palette would otherwise read as "asset pack", not "majlis".
